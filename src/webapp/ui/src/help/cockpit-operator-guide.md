# Cockpit Operator Guide

Cockpit is your live triage screen. It is designed to help you decide where to act next.

## What this page is for

Use Cockpit when you need a live operational picture with direct links into the pages that can resolve the issue.

## What to do here

1. Scan current risk and approval signals.
2. Identify the most urgent intervention path.
3. Open the destination page that can actually resolve the issue.

## Why this matters

Cockpit helps you prioritize. It does not replace Sessions, Approvals, Agents, or Observability for detailed work.
