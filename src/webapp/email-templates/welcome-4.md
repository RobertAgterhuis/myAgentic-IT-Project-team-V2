# Sprint 1: Real Results

Theory is nice. Results are better. Here's what the Agentic SDLC Platform
delivered in its first production sprint.

## Sprint 1 by the Numbers

| Metric                   | Result                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sprint Duration**      | 14 days (March 10–24, 2026)                                                 |
| **Items Planned**        | 15                                                                          |

## What Got Built

- **Governance framework** with audit trail and stakeholder sign-off process
- **CI/CD pipeline** with 8-job GitHub Actions workflow (security scanning,
  Docker builds, smoke tests, accessibility gate)
- **Design token system** with WCAG AA compliance verification
- **Brand identity** with design tokens, style guide, and component inventory
- **GTM messaging framework** with 4-pillar content strategy
- **Email marketing framework** with 5-email welcome sequence spec
- **Analytics baseline** with privacy-first Matomo deployment plan

## The Deferred Items

Two items were intentionally deferred to Sprint 2:

1. **TMS Setup (SP-2-501)** — Weblate evaluation needed more time
2. **Analytics Deployment (SP-2-201)** — Matomo infrastructure in Sprint 2

Both were deferred through the formal Sprint Gate process — not swept under the
rug. Transparent tracking is how teams build trust.

## The Retrospective Insight

> "Front-loading design and analysis in Phases 1–4 meant implementation had
> clear specs for every item. Zero ambiguity at sprint start."
