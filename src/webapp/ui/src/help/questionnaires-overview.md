# Questionnaire Workflow

Questionnaires capture missing decisions and constraints needed by phase agents.

## When questionnaires appear

They are generated when required context is missing or decisions remain unresolved.

## Answering guidance

Prioritize required questions and keep answers specific and actionable.

## Re-evaluation flow

After significant answer updates, trigger re-evaluation so downstream outputs reflect the new context.
