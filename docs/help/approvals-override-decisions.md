# Override Decisions and Audit

Overrides allow progression despite normal blocking conditions, but require durable accountability.

## Minimum audit record

- Decision identifier.
- Blocking condition being overridden.
- Risk statement and mitigation.
- Approver and timestamp.
- Expiration or review date.

## Review model

- Re-validate the override at the next gate or planned checkpoint.
- Close or renew with updated rationale.
- Ensure linked artifacts remain discoverable for compliance review.
