# Questionnaire Operator Guide

Questionnaires exist to remove ambiguity. Their value comes from answer quality, not from answer volume.

## What this page is for

Use Questionnaires to supply missing information that agents cannot safely infer.

## What to do here

1. Answer required questions first.
2. Be concrete about scope, constraints, and decisions.
3. Leave unknown items open or deferred rather than inventing certainty.
4. Trigger re-evaluation after material changes.

## Why this matters

Weak answers propagate weak reasoning. Strong answers reduce rework, repeated questions, and policy conflicts later in the pipeline.

## Good operator habit

Answer in operational language. Say what must happen, what must not happen, and what tradeoff is acceptable.
