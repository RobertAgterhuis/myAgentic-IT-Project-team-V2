# Identity Consent Center Guide

Identity Consent Center helps you answer a simple question: what access has been granted, and is that access still justified?

## What to do here

1. Review granted scopes and delegated permissions.
2. Confirm which supported workflows actually require them.
3. Remove or challenge access that no longer has a clear operational reason.

## Why this matters

Unused access is still risk. This page keeps identity permissions intentional and reviewable.
