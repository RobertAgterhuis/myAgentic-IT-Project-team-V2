Audit records provide accountability for governed actions.

## What is logged

Events typically include actor identity, operation, resource, and timestamp.

## Filtering strategy

Filter by actor, operation type, and resource to isolate incidents quickly.

## Evidence export

Use exports for compliance reviews and post-incident analysis.
