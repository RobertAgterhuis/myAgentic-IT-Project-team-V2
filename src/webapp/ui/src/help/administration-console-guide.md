# Administration Console Guide

Administration is where platform-wide settings become policy. Changes here can affect every user and every run.

## What this page is for

Use Administration when you need to govern the system itself rather than a single workflow execution.

## What to do here

1. Confirm what business or operational problem the change is meant to solve.
2. Review impact radius before making the change.
3. Prefer the narrowest possible control that solves the problem.

## Why this matters

Administration changes are high leverage. That means they are powerful, but also risky when made casually.
