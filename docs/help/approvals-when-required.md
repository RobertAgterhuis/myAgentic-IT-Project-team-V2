# When Approvals Are Required

Approvals are required when work intersects governance, security, compliance, or policy exceptions.

## Common approval triggers

- Policy deviations or exception requests.
- Production-impacting changes with elevated risk.
- Security-sensitive configuration changes.
- Cross-team decisions with contractual or regulatory impact.

## Decision hygiene

Capture the request, rationale, approver identity, and final decision status in the governance decision trail.
