# Artifacts Operator Guide

Artifacts are the concrete outputs of the workflow. This page helps you decide whether the pipeline produced what it was supposed to produce.

## What this page is for

Use Artifacts to verify deliverables, inspect evidence, and check whether required outputs exist before review or progression.

## What to do here

1. Filter by phase, type, or status.
2. Confirm required deliverables are present.
3. Open lineage when you need to understand provenance or impact.

## Why this matters

A workflow can appear complete while still missing critical evidence. This page is where you catch that before a gate, approval, or release step depends on incomplete output.
